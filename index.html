About Greenwood

Greenwood Public Library is a free, shared space for reading, learning and meeting neighbours. Our collection grows with the town, and our doors stay open to anyone who wants to step in, sit down and stay a while.

A library built by the town, for the town.

Greenwood Public Library opened in the early eighties with a few hundred donated books and a borrowed table. Neighbours brought what they had finished reading, and the shelves slowly filled.

Today the collection holds more than twelve thousand titles across six sections, from picture books to local history. Every item is chosen with the people who use it in mind.

The building is more than a store of books. Students revise at the long tables, parents bring toddlers to story time, and retired readers meet for coffee after the morning papers arrive.

Membership is free for residents, and so is the wifi. If you are new to town, bring a piece of mail with your address and we will have your card ready in minutes.
